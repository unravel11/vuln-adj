# RQ2 Post-profile Eligible-universe CWE-difference Evidence Contract v1

## Scope

This audit covers all 29 `cwe_ids` rows where `current` and
`cwe_taxonomy_v1` differ in the complete 5,948-CVE snapshot-external eligible
universe. It is outcome-complete for that deterministic impact set, not
field-complete for all 5,948 CWE rows and not a sample.

The snapshot, profiles, prediction census, prior 250-row reviews, and three
overlapping all-50 CWE decisions are already revealed. The result is therefore
a post-hoc, same-snapshot, non-human development diagnostic. It cannot become
human gold or confirmatory evidence.

## Frozen identity and selection

- Bind the census manifest, analysis, difference JSONL, parent cohort manifest,
  field views, aligned rows, CWE 4.20 Research Concepts archive, contract,
  prompt, builder, runner, merge, verifier, evidence fetcher, and helper code.
- Independently require exactly 29 unique CVEs with field `cwe_ids`, current
  prediction `factual_conflict`, and taxonomy prediction
  `representation_discrepancy`.
- Evidence availability, prior review status, and expected direction may not
  remove a row.
- The three CVEs already present in the sealed 250-row all-50 audit remain in
  the 29-row denominator; any repeat agreement is same-model stability only.

## Evidence and worklists

- Reconstruct each source row from the census-bound field view and aligned
  record, including raw CWE sets, vulnerability summaries, official CWE entries,
  and allowed ancestor/descendant paths.
- Select up to three URLs already listed by NVD or GHSA using the frozen ranking
  function. Freeze source URL, derived fetch URL, source membership, fetch
  status, timestamp, title, snippet, and cache-file hash under a new cache
  directory. Do not refresh evidence during review.
- Zero-success rows remain in the denominator and must abstain unless the
  supplied structured context itself satisfies the strict contract.
- Reviewer worklists must omit baseline status/note, every profile name and
  prediction, changed-profile membership, prior reviewers/consensus, gold,
  correctness, sampling stratum, design weight, and selection reason.
- All 29 rows share a visible disjoint taxonomy relation with official paths;
  therefore `selection_blinding_complete=false` even when prediction values are
  absent. No same-snapshot filler controls are introduced.

## Review contract

- Reviewer E reasons set relation, then taxonomy, then mechanism. Reviewer F
  reasons mechanism, then taxonomy, then set relation.
- Roles use exact reverse worklist order, distinct reviewer/run/session sets,
  Codex CLI `gpt-5.5`, medium reasoning, read-only ephemeral sessions, and
  batches of at most five.
- A determinate non-exact label must cite a successful frozen URL and copy a
  literal 20--280 character substring from its snippet. Quotes are validated
  before merge.
- Strict consensus requires exact agreement on set relation, discrepancy label,
  taxonomy compatibility, and specific mapping verdict; both confidences must
  be high/medium and neither role may request further review.
- Any disagreement remains unresolved. No third same-model vote is allowed.

## Analysis and claim boundary

Report all 29 rows, strict/unresolved counts, candidate/current/neither
directions, effective correctness-discordant rows, and the conditional exact
two-sided McNemar diagnostic. Keep the full 29-row denominator visible.

- `uses_any_labels=true`, `uses_human_labels=false`, and
  `label_is_human=false` after review.
- No absolute accuracy, macro-F1, prevalence, independent-human agreement,
  temporal generalization, confirmatory gain, preregistered power, candidate
  promotion, or production switch is allowed.
- The sealed 250-row evaluation and every real-person review requirement remain
  unchanged.
