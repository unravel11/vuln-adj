\section{Conclusion}

This paper studies what remains after CVE-ID alignment: structured fields can still disagree in ways that require different downstream actions. The aligned NVD-GHSA corpus shows substantial field-level discrepancy after matching, and the RQ2 diagnostics show that most binary differences are not routed to deterministic factual-conflict candidates. A 1,250-CVE exposure-disjoint typing-stability cohort reaches 0.9176 strict same-model consensus coverage. A post-unseal third blind pass raises non-human candidate coverage to 0.9704 but fails its gate. A subsequent frozen-URL D/E audit resolves only 6 of the remaining 37 non-human rows and raises coverage to 0.9752, again below fixed thresholds; affected\_versions remains 0/28. A targeted audit of the remaining two CWE rows and one references row supports one additional development mechanism candidate but promotes none because the cohort and evidence routes are already revealed. An immutable-log frontier reconstructs 2,767 request row-attempts, including 90 retry row-items, and shows marginal selected-row yield falling from 66/103 at C to 6/37 at D/E; the fixed stop rule rejects further same-model escalation on this cohort. A later official snapshot contains no strict event-time CVE but supports a 250-row snapshot-external development cohort with 0.9240 strict dual-Codex coverage. Only three rows distinguish sealed profiles, and only one has strict consensus favoring the CWE candidate in the main review. A post-selection three-case frozen-evidence diagnostic makes two fresh same-family passes agree with the candidate direction on all three cases. Expanding the same diagnostic to all 50 CWE rows retains 49 strict decisions and yields 45/49 current versus 48/49 candidate agreement, but field selection remains post-hoc. A final 16-row non-CWE evidence stage resolves only four rows despite complete fetch availability; its staged 238/250 candidate passes coverage but fails the fixed 0.40 resolution gate and leaves the sealed evaluation unchanged. A label-free enumeration proves that the three profile-difference rows bound any full-cohort accuracy difference to +/-1.2 percentage points under every possible five-label assignment. All six profiles collapse to two vectors on that sample, and no possible label assignment can make their exact two-sided paired comparison significant at alpha 0.05; the minimum attainable p-value is 0.25. A prediction-only census over all 5,948 eligible CVEs instead separates all six vectors but finds only 34 difference CVEs across 29,740 field instances: 29 CWE and at most five references differences. This revealed-universe result corrects the sampled-rate assumption and informs disagreement-enriched future design; without labels it does not show correctness or gain. None of the evidence stages permits promotion. Complete-impact references and CWE audits expose additional bounded candidate directions and construct disagreements. The affected\_versions holdouts separate discrepancy typing from source adjudication and find no stable method improvement. All current annotation-backed comparisons remain non-human rather than human-gold performance results.

The main methodological lesson is type-first: post-alignment fusion needs a discrepancy type before it needs a winner. Many field differences are better handled as normalization, incompleteness, or temporal interpretation, and only residual factual conflicts should be passed to evidence-constrained source-support analysis.

The present contribution is therefore a scoped task definition, taxonomy, auditable baseline, evidence-handoff scaffold, and leakage-controlled, task-separated evaluation protocol with explicit failure, protocol-drift, evidence-dependence, event-time eligibility, and prediction-identifiability analysis. It is not validated as an adjudication method: the v2 type candidate is too selective, the source method does not beat a fixed-source comparator, and the RQ2 post-hoc contract profile reverses direction across protocol generations. Two disjoint AI calibrations refine the candidate contract and stabilize four clauses, but the full gate still rejects it. A targeted residual audit shows that frozen source code can support one local CWE mechanism while official mapping validity and URL-resource identity leave two cases unresolved; its one development \texttt{factual\_conflict} is not promoted. For the remaining XWiki row, a lineage-aware graph binds product dependencies and artifact migration and derives an \texttt{incomplete} development candidate with one missing prerelease. A subsequent generic contract binds eight equal-boundary artifact-mismatch cases, but a fixed non-equal test reaches only 4/5 projection coverage and 2/5 consistency with both sealed AI reviewers. A frozen snapshot-extensional Codex candidate projects two homogeneous InLong components but disagrees with both reviewers. A label-independent full-aligned-data cohort across NuGet, PyPI, and crates.io initially reaches 0/3 product projections because package catalogs and dependency constraints lack total product-release mappings. A fixed post-no-go Deno diagnostic then recovers one such edge from 71 official tag lockfiles and derives an NVD-strict-subset, non-human \texttt{incomplete} candidate with five runtime-only releases. By contrast, Mattermost and EVE each yield 0/2 project-family projections, while the Hutool mechanism projects two development rows and six same-snapshot exposure-disjoint rows without promotion. Together these cases show why edge-specific build evidence can recover one sequence while repository identity, valid pseudo syntax, commit timestamps, or current catalog equality alone cannot establish an approved historical range construct. Completing validation still requires a human-approved range contract and edge-class-specific mapping evidence, followed by two real reviewers and author signoff over both prepared 1,250-row and post-profile 250-row blind RQ2 packets and the RQ3 human audit. A future confirmation cohort must require both NVD and reviewed-GHSA event timestamps after the frozen profile, census frozen predictions before label collection, and separate disagreement-focused paired testing from probability-sampled absolute evaluation; a later download alone is insufficient.
