\title{Field-Level Discrepancy Typing across Aligned Vulnerability Databases}
\author{TODO Author}
\address{TODO affiliation}
\begin{abstract}
Vulnerability records are often aligned by CVE identifier before they are used for security analytics, but alignment does not make their field values agree. This paper studies post-alignment field-level discrepancies between NVD and GHSA records. From 100,032 normalized NVD records and 28,785 normalized GHSA records, the current corpus contains 8,066 CVE-ID aligned pairs. The central design is type-first: a deterministic baseline assigns each target field to one of five working discrepancy types before any source-support question is considered. The resulting RQ1 distribution shows that disagreement is field-specific: severity and affected\_versions retain visible factual-conflict tails, published/date is dominated by representation and temporal differences, references are mostly incomplete, and cwe\_ids are mostly equivalent. We then report non-gold RQ2 rule-trigger and annotation-readiness diagnostics, plus silver-label RQ3 evidence diagnostics for severity and affected\_versions. These artifacts characterize evidence availability, source provenance, threshold sensitivity, and audit targets; they are not human gold and are not final evaluation claims. The main contribution is therefore a scoped task definition, field-aware discrepancy taxonomy, and reproducible baseline artifact for structured vulnerability-data discrepancy typing, with an evidence-constrained adjudication scaffold whose validation is left to the pending gold-label phase.
\end{abstract}
