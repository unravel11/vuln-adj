## RQ2 rule-trigger diagnostic

The planned RQ2 performance evaluation requires a human discrepancy-typing gold set and is not yet complete. As an interim paper-facing diagnostic, `results/rq2_discrepancy_typing/rq2_typing_diagnostics.md` summarizes which deterministic rules fire on the current field-view artifact. This diagnostic should be read as audit prioritization, not as accuracy, precision, recall, or macro-F1.

Across 40,330 field instances, 29,986 are binary-different under a coarse same/different view. Of those binary-different instances, 27,499 (91.7%) are assigned to non-conflict categories: representation_discrepancy, incomplete, or temporal_discrepancy. Only 2,487 (8.3%) are assigned to factual_conflict by the deterministic baseline. This supports the paper's design choice to type disagreements before adjudication; it does not prove that the assigned labels are correct.

The largest rule triggers are also useful for audit planning: references subset incompleteness accounts for 7,734 instances, same-day publication timestamp representation differences for 6,169 instances, same canonical severity labels for 3,173 instances, affected_versions one-sided presence for 2,845 instances, and affected_versions effective-bound representation equivalence for 2,303 instances. These counts indicate where gold labeling should focus, especially before turning RQ2 into a performance claim.

The RQ2 sample-readiness diagnostic at `results/rq2_discrepancy_typing/rq2_sample_coverage.md` checks the annotation templates themselves. The primary seed contains 300 rows, with 60 rows for each target field; the 20% consistency template contains 60 rows, with 12 rows per field. All 17 nonzero `(field, baseline_status)` candidate strata are sampled, and the primary seed covers the top 12 full-corpus deterministic rule triggers. All 300 primary `manual_status` cells and all 60 reviewer labels are blank, so this remains a coverage/readiness result rather than evidence of typing accuracy or annotation agreement.

The exact paired-test diagnostic further shows that the six sealed profiles collapse to two prediction-vector equivalence classes: current and both reference profiles are identical, while the CWE profile and both combined profiles are identical. Every cross-class comparison has the same three prediction-difference rows. Across the 125 possible labels on those rows, the number of correctness-discordant pairs is 0, 1, 2, or 3 in 27, 54, 36, and 8 assignments, respectively. Conditional on already obtaining correctness-discordant rows, 0.80 exact power requires 49, 20, or 12 such rows when the candidate-direction win probability is assumed to be 0.70, 0.80, or 0.90. These are design sensitivities, not validated rates, preregistered sample sizes, or evidence of method gain.
