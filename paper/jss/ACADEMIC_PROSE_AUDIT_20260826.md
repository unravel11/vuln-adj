# Academic Prose Audit

**Scope:** complete English zero draft in `manuscript.md` and synchronized
`latex/main.tex`
**Method:** `academic-humanizer` semantic, terminology, contrast, and
document-pattern contracts
**Decision:** `PASS_WITH_RESULT_PLACEHOLDERS`

This audit evaluates observable prose defects. It does not estimate authorship,
optimize for a detector, or authorize claims beyond the evidence ledger.

## Claim and terminology locks

- The title, RQ1/RQ2/RQ3, three contribution ceilings, corpus sizes, field
  counts, strategy counts, comparison directions, thresholds, and Branch P/B
  conditions were treated as locked content.
- Canonical terms are `field-aware simple comparator`, `current type-first
  candidate`, `abstention-aware type-first candidate`, `conflict escalation`,
  `abstention`, and `total manual route`.
- `factual conflict` is a deterministic status; `conflict escalation` is an
  action. They remain distinct.
- `trained analyst` is preserved. The prose does not promote the construct to
  practitioner judgment or human gold.

## Findings and edits

1. The draft did not contain promotional novelty language, unsupported causal
   claims, or decorative significance claims. No sentence was changed merely to
   vary rhythm.
2. Claim-boundary sentences such as “not source truth,” “not workload,” and “not
   a reproduced baseline” were retained where they distinguish concrete
   constructs that a reader could otherwise conflate. These are functional scope
   statements, not unsupported rhetorical contrasts.
3. Repeated project-process language was kept outside the clean paper body.
   Result placeholders remain visible because the author explicitly requires a
   zero draft that cannot preselect RQ3 or a result branch.
4. The hand-maintained provisional reference list was removed from Markdown.
   The draft now points to one checked BibTeX source and a citation/evidence map,
   reducing citation drift without changing the related-work claims.
5. Exact RQ1/RQ2 tables were added from the deterministic source. Their notes use
   the same terms and evidence ceiling as the body.

## Residual boundaries

- The abstract, RQ3 result, conclusion branch, author metadata, declarations,
  public archive identifiers, and final highlights remain unresolved by design.
- Citation metadata closure does not prove that the 24-paper archive is
  exhaustive.
- A later author revision may change voice and emphasis, but it must preserve the
  claim and terminology locks above.
