## Evidence combination rule

E01–E07E may support a bounded empirical audit, policy-disagreement census,
packet-design decision, mechanical workflow claim, and negative/failure
narrative. They may not be combined to simulate E08 or E09. V3.1 uses one
bounded human collection process for both RQ2 and RQ3, but both human evidence
and the policy comparison remain missing until independent returns and gates
exist.
