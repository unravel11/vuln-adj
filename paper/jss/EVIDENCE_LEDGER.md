## Evidence combination rule

E01/E07B support deterministic RQ1/RQ2 claims at the stated snapshot and
pipeline ceiling. E01–E07F may also support packet design and mechanical
workflow claims, but they may not be combined to simulate E08 or E09. V3.1 uses
one bounded human collection process for RQ3 only; every analyst-backed strategy
result remains missing until independent returns and gates exist. E13 preserves
the superseded lock. E14 supports only the governance fact that the current
routing-centric argument is author locked; it cannot promote any empirical
claim. E15 supports only the result-independent manuscript and artifact
mechanics; it cannot promote a deterministic allocation into a semantic or
submission claim.
