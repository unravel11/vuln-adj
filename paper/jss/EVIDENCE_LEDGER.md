## Evidence combination rule

E01–E07C may support a bounded empirical audit, policy-disagreement census,
packet-design decision, and negative/failure narrative. They may not be
combined to simulate E08 or E09. V3 uses one human collection round for both
RQ2 and RQ3, but both human evidence and the policy comparison remain missing
until the independent returns and frozen analysis gates exist.
